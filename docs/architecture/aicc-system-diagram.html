AICC — AI Call Center Platform · System at a glance

PRD Draft v1 · Sean Stott · 2026-07-27 · FiveStrata / AutoWeb internal

The decision: a complete, self-contained AI call center. Supabase runs the operational brain (queue, routing, controls), Telnyx carries the calls, and an AI agent replaces the soundboard operator — canned clips first, TTS for the long tail. No ViciDial instance; its vocabulary (dispositions, lists, campaigns) is retained so results compare one-to-one with the human floors. No human screener or closer anywhere in the call path.

Solid arrows: lead/call flow. Dashed arrows: control plane — DID manager supplies numbers to the dialer, client selection feeds the warm-transfer leg, dispositions write back to techss_ keyed by OLeadID (MDB and existing dashboards keep working). Supabase hot tables (30–90 d) ETL nightly into Snowflake; recordings archive independently and land there too. Source: fivestrata-dialer/docs/PRD.md §2.
