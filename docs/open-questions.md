Implication for the ≤~200ms gate: Call Control playback alone won't hit it — either **concatenate fixed clip sequences at authoring time** (no seam at all within an agent turn) or run audio ourselves via **media streaming** (the BYO half of the W1 bake-off, now sharply posed). Note the turn-taking seam (caller stops → agent clip starts) has a natural human budget of 200–500ms, which reactive-path co-location (step 3) plausibly reaches; the *intra-turn* clip seam is the one that must be ~0.
  **Round 2 (8/7 pm, conversational loop — `scripts/convo-loop-test.ts`):** (a) **MP3 clips cut the pre-queued seam to ~320ms** (vs ~550ms WAV — container, not sample rate, was the variable); (b) **Telnyx hosted AI works under our existing API key** — 25 LLMs (`/v2/ai/chat/completions`, Llama 3.3-70B etc.), TTS file generation (`/v2/text-to-speech/speech`, 4,450 voices incl.   **Round 3 (8/7 evening, calls 5–9 — live Sean-feedback iteration):** turn seam **1597ms → 465ms** across five calls. What did it: transcription starts at `call.answered` not after the greeting (catches immediate replies, kills engine spin-up from the perceived gap); **Deepgram engine** (engine B finals took 3–5s; Deepgram ~1.9s) **with `interim_results: true`** — the ack fires on the caller's *partial* transcript, before endpointing finalizes (biggest single win, seam 1300→465ms); ack on first sign of speech, LLM waits for the final. UX findings that generalize into the clip taxonomy: ultra-short clips ("Mm hm") arrive **garbled over telephony** — acks must be real 1–2s phrases; **connectors vs stalls** — "one second" promises a pause and is incongruous before immediate speech, so wait-promising clips are a separate category the engine fires only when the decision is actually slow; **seam tolerance is asymmetric** — callers are least tolerant of dead air right after answering a direct question; **variant rotation must avoid immediate repeats** (same ack twice in one call reads robotic — the PRD's 3–5-variant rule needs a no-repeat constraint). Remaining gap ≈ Deepgram partial latency (~1–2s) + dev-loop 465–790ms; co-location (step 3) targets the latter → near-human turn-taking without media streaming.
